How it works

One workflow.
Every handoff handled.

Beyond the schedule

Modules for the whole department.

The command center runs your daily operations. These modules extend it to the rest of the job, each one included in your plan when you need it.

Eligibility

Available now

Registration to roster, automatically.

Online registration with paperwork, physicals, and clearances collected at signup, and athletes rostered automatically as they clear. Eligibility keeps checking all season (grades, standing, missing forms) with one-touch output of every required list.

Facilities

Available now

Every space, every level of management.

Games, practices, and community rentals across every field, gym, and pool you run. Shared-site conflicts surface before they become double-bookings, from varsity down to middle school.

Medical

Available now

Fully integrated, built for your staff.

Medical is fully integrated into the site: individual recordkeeping and care plans for every athlete, injury logs, and return-to-play status, kept by your training staff without a second system.

Officials & Payments

Coming soon

Assigning through payout.

Full referee management, from assignments and availability to paying officials and game workers, in the same place the schedule already lives.

Every module shares the same schedule, rosters, and communication, so nothing is entered twice.
