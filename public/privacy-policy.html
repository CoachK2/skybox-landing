Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 26, 2026 Last Updated: March 26, 2026 Row9 LLC
Overview

Introduction

Row9 LLC ("Row9," "we," "us," or "our") operates Skybox, an athletics operations and communications platform designed for high school athletic directors and their programs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information in connection with your use of Skybox and related services.

By using Skybox, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

Skybox is a managed service platform. Row9 LLC operates all system functions on behalf of participating schools and athletic programs. End users (athletic directors, staff) interact via email and SMS notifications managed by Row9.

Section 1

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information in connection with our services:

  • Contact Information: Names, email addresses, and phone numbers of athletic directors, coaches, school administrators, and opposing school contacts provided by participating schools or sourced from publicly available athletic directories.
  • Game and Schedule Data: Athletic event details including dates, times, locations, sport types, and opponent information.
  • Communication Records: Email and SMS correspondence related to game confirmations, postponements, and operational notifications sent through the Skybox platform.
  • Usage Data: System logs, dashboard access records, and interaction data related to the Skybox platform.
  • Officials Data: Referee and official assignment data sourced from licensed third-party officiating platforms (e.g., Arbiter Sports) under applicable terms.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial account details, or student education records (FERPA-protected data).

Section 2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send game confirmation, postponement, and operational alert communications to athletic directors and school staff
  • Provide SMS notifications to consenting recipients regarding athletic event updates
  • Maintain accurate records of game confirmations and communications for audit purposes
  • Improve and develop the Skybox platform and related services
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations
Section 3

SMS Communications

Skybox sends SMS notifications related to athletic event operations, including game confirmations, postponement alerts, and official assignment notifications. By providing a phone number in connection with Skybox services, you consent to receive these operational messages.

  • Message frequency varies based on athletic event schedules and operational needs
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS notifications
  • Reply HELP for assistance or contact us at coach@kerrykeating.com

We do not send marketing or promotional SMS messages. All SMS communications are strictly transactional and operational in nature.

Section 4

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not sell or share your information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purpose outside of service delivery. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party platforms including Twilio (SMS delivery), AgentMail (email delivery), Railway (cloud hosting), and Anthropic (AI processing) to operate the Skybox platform. These providers process data solely to deliver our services.
  • Participating Schools: Game and confirmation data is shared with the applicable school's athletic staff as part of the service.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
Section 5

Data Retention

We retain game records, confirmation logs, and communication data for the duration of each pilot program and for a reasonable period thereafter for audit and compliance purposes. Contact information is retained as long as necessary to provide services to participating schools.

You may request deletion of your information by contacting us at coach@kerrykeating.com.

Section 6

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. These measures include encrypted data transmission, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 7

Children's Privacy

Skybox is designed for use by school athletic administrators and staff. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Section 8

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify participating schools of material changes. Continued use of Skybox after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
